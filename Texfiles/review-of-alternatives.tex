\chapter{REVIEW OF EXISTING ALTERNATIVES}
\label{chap:review-of-alternatives}

This chapter evaluates current market solutions, generic digital applications, and legacy manual workflows utilized to address domain operational needs. A comparative assessment highlights the technical and operational gaps of existing options, establishing the justification for the proposed \textbf{GoForth} system.

\section{Evaluation of Existing Alternatives}

\subsection{Direct Commercial Solutions}
Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) platforms provide pre-built enterprise functionality and established compliance features. However, these systems present significant constraints for targeted deployment:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{High Cost Overhead:} Recurring per-user licensing costs create prohibitive financial barriers.
    \item \textbf{Workflow Inflexibility:} Fixed feature sets cannot adapt to localized organizational workflows.
    \item \textbf{Integration Limits:} Restricted programmatic access prevents custom API integrations.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Generic Digital Tools}
Organizations frequently adapt productivity applications (e.g., spreadsheets and basic messaging tools) to plug operational gaps. Key limitations include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Data Integrity Risks:} High susceptibility to manual entry errors and inconsistent formatting.
    \item \textbf{Lack of Automation:} Absence of automated validation routines, dynamic notifications, and real-time synchronization.
    \item \textbf{Security Vulnerabilities:} Inadequate role-based access control (RBAC) and lack of formal audit logging.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Manual / Legacy Workflows}
Traditional paper-based record-keeping and verbal coordination remain prevalent due to minimal setup requirements. Primary drawbacks include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{High Latency:} Paper-bound approvals and manual filing slow operational throughput.
    \item \textbf{Risk of Physical Loss:} Physical files are vulnerable to damage, misplacement, and unauthorized access.
    \item \textbf{Absence of Analytics:} Inability to generate aggregate operational metrics or real-time reports.
\end{itemize}

\section{Gap Analysis and System Rationale}

\subsection{Can You Use These Alternatives Instead?}
While commercial platforms offer robust functionality, their high recurring cost and rigid architecture prevent full alignment with localized requirements. Generic tools and manual processes offer low entry barriers but fail to maintain data integrity, security, and real-time processing capabilities. Consequently, existing options cannot be used as direct replacements.

\subsection{Rationale for GoForth}
Developing the proposed \textbf{GoForth} system is necessary due to the following factors:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Biggest Gap in Existing Systems:} No single existing solution offers specialized domain-specific workflows combined with automated real-time validation and low-cost deployment.
    \item \textbf{Why Our Project is Worth Developing:} \textbf{GoForth} unifies disparate manual and generic digital processes into a single, cohesive platform, eliminating operational latency and data redundancy.
    \item \textbf{Our Unique Contribution:} An extensible, role-secured digital management framework optimized specifically for targeted operational environments.
\end{itemize}
